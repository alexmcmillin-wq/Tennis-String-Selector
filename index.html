How we pick your tension

We start from a baseline for the string material and then adjust for you and your racquet. Lower tension gives more power, comfort and spin; higher tension gives more control.

Gauge

Thinner strings (17 / 1.24 mm) bite the ball more and feel crisper; thicker strings (16 / 1.30 mm) last longer. Spin hitters usually prefer thin poly unless they break it in a few hours, in which case go up to 16L or 16.

Recommendations are a starting point. String ratings are relative, based on lab data and player reviews. Fine-tune tension by 2 lbs at a time.
